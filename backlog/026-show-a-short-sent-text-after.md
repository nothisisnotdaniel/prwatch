# 026 Show a short sent text after a Consent, Merge or Cancel tap

Category: ux

## What

After a successful Consent, Merge or Cancel tap, the web app shows nothing until the reload shows a "requested" line. Show a short fixed text ("Sent") on the PR right away.

## Why

review-ux 2 of job 426: without it the user can't tell that the tap worked and may tap again.
